# Bounty Rules: Appeal Fees and Staking

This document outlines the specific rules regarding appeal fees and worker staking that were introduced in ProofWorks Phase 9.

## Appeal Fees
When participating in bounties, an appeal bond is required to dispute outcomes. The appeal bond is strictly set to **20% of the task reward**.

## Worker Staking
To ensure commitment and timely delivery, workers are required to stake tokens when claiming a task. **Worker stake is forfeited if the claim expires without a proof submission.** Please ensure you submit your proof before the deadline to avoid losing your staked tokens.
