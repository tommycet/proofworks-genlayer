# Bounty Rules

## Appeal Bond

When a bounty claim is disputed, the appealing party must post an **appeal bond equal to 20% of the task reward**.

- The bond is held in escrow until the appeal is resolved.
- If the appeal succeeds, the bond is returned.
- If the appeal fails, the bond is forfeited to cover review costs.

## Worker Staking

Workers who claim a bounty must stake tokens proportional to the task reward.

- If a claim **expires without a proof submission**, the worker's stake is **forfeited**.
- Staking ensures workers only claim tasks they intend to complete.
- Upon successful submission and approval, the stake is returned along with the reward.
